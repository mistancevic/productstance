How I decide what is worth building

Worth Building treats product work as a chain of decisions, in this order. Each one needs evidence and ends in a verdict: go on, change course, or go back a step.

  1. 1 · Direction

    Where do we play?

    Align with the business first: which market, which customers, which goal.

  2. 2 · Discovery

    What are we solving, for whom?

    Find the real problem and the people who have it, with evidence from them.

  3. 3 · Worth solving

    Is this problem worth solving?

    Is it big enough for them and for the business, and is the evidence strong enough to act on?

  4. 4 · Worth building

    Is this worth building?

    Only then the solution: if it works, what changes for the customer or the business?

AI changes the work between these decisions. The decisions stay with people: choosing the problem, judging the evidence and setting the quality bar.
